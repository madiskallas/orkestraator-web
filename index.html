Orkestraator OÜ

A software development company registered in Estonia. We design and build software, and we are currently making apps for Apple Watch and iPhone.

What we do

Orkestraator OÜ provides software development services: working out what a piece of software has to do, designing it, building it and keeping it running. The company has been in the Estonian Business Register since 2013.

Our own product is VMG Watch, an app for racing sailors. It turns an Apple Watch Ultra into a racing instrument: boat speed, speed made good towards the wind, a start countdown you can feel on the wrist, and a GPS track of every race saved to Apple Fitness. It is in development and is tested on the water before each release.

How we build

  • Privacy first. Our apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of their own. What they record stays on the user's devices. See the privacy policy.
  • Built for the real conditions. VMG Watch is designed for wet hands, bright sun and a locked touch screen, and it is tested on a real watch on the water, not only in a simulator.
  • Platform-native. We use Apple's own frameworks — HealthKit, Core Location, App Intents — rather than third-party SDKs.
